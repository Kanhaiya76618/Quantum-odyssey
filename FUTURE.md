# FUTURE — ideas cut for scope, kept for the roadmap

Deliberate cuts from the Qubit City interactive-builder milestone (kept the 60fps budget honest):

- **Simulation energy wave** — on each run, a cyan pulse travels every wire control→target, lighting gates as it passes, ending in a Quantum Core super-pulse. Needs a small timeline orchestrator over the serialized gate order.
- **Eigen connection beams** — when Eigen references a gate, a light line briefly travels from the gate to the hologram (and a vertical hologram beam + rising particles above the gate).
- **Handheld camera drift** — breathing/drift layered on OrbitControls without fighting user input (needs an offset rig group around the camera).
- **Procedural nebula + galaxies** — shader-based volumetric background (current build uses a star shell + fog to stay in budget, no textures).
- **Gate placement electric arcs** — tiny lightning between a new gate and its rail (line segments with jittered points, fading).
- **Floor as live processor** — animated circuit traces, hex hologram sublayer, probability waves, blinking nodes (currently: glass disc + holo rings + neon grid).
- **Success supernova** — full choreography on run success: core burst, floor light race, particle swirl, bloom peak, settle.
- **Runes/glyphs + scanlines on Eigen** — floating glyph particles and holographic scanline shader.
- **Shooting stars / lens dust** — occasional streak sprites and subtle lens artifacts.
- **Voice pulse rings** — expanding torus shells from Eigen synced to typewriter cadence.
